

BEFORE YOU DECIDE
In your reasoning, compare two or three candidate changes: the one you will make and at least one alternative that you decide against. For each candidate write one line, exactly in this form:
FORECAST: VERB(arg, ...) -> better
The word after the arrow is your forecast of what the simulator returns for that change alone, applied to the current design. It is one of:
refused - the simulator rejects the change and the design stays as it is
better - the change is applied and the design is closer than it is now to meeting every constraint at low objective cost (feasibility first, then the objective)
worse - the change is applied and the design is not closer
Write each candidate exactly as an action is written, with every argument. Then make the candidate you forecast to do best, in the one final action line as described above.
